# CONTENT_WRITING.md — EDITORIAL VOICE & CONTENT OPERATIONS

## Editorial thesis
RayMindset documents a person in motion: building, learning, training, traveling, questioning, and trying again. The writing must earn authority through specificity and reflection, not self-awarded status.

## Voice
First person for Ray's story and essays; direct, curious, confident, self-aware, occasionally playful. Avoid corporate “visionary entrepreneur,” fake wisdom, hustle clichés, billionaire manifestation slogans, and repetitive inspirational one-liners. Keep sentences varied and human.

## Content pillars
1. Building: companies, experiments, failures, leadership, operations.
2. Discipline: boxing, training, consistency, discomfort.
3. Exploration: Asia backpacking, international travel, human encounters.
4. Ideas: healthcare, technology, AI, human behavior, business futures.
5. Writing: *The Suitcase of Memories*, essays, stories, reflections.
6. The Next Chapter: ambitions and questions, always labeled as exploration.

## About page structure
Opening scene / childhood or early influence (only after interview) / travel and writing / discipline and boxing / beginnings in business / Socialsect and healthcare / lessons from setbacks / ideas and next questions / open ending. Interview Ray for concrete moments rather than filling with generic narrative.

## Factual control
Mark all statements as Verified / Needs Ray confirmation / Editorial draft / Future possibility. Never use private company numbers, patient data, employee names, personal finance, or sensitive personal details by default. Confirm the book title, publication, and approved excerpts. Seek explicit approval before naming clients/doctors or using likenesses.

## SEO/editorial
Publish a substantial /story page, clear author attribution, Article/Person structured data when applicable, canonical URLs, meaningful headings, human summaries, original images with alt text, and internal links. Write for readers first; no keyword stuffing or promises of AI ranking.

## Initial content production checklist
60-minute founder interview; approved 12–20 milestone timeline; 10–20 authentic photos; 3–5 boxing clips; travel archive; book cover/metadata; 1 strong founding essay; 3 initial journal posts; Socialsect verified venture profile; verified contact/social URLs.
